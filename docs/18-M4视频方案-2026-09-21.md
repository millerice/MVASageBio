## 2. 逐字稿 v1（英文；425 词；朗读节奏见 §5）

**S1 (0:00–0:15)**
Mosaic variegated aneuploidy syndrome is so rare that for the child behind this dataset, there are
zero registered clinical trials. Not few. Zero — re-verified live against the registry the week we
finished this work. This is our three-minute account of what can still be done.

**S2 (0:15–0:40)**
In this child, the cause is biallelic loss of BUB1B — the gene encoding BUBR1, a spindle assembly
checkpoint kinase. Without enough BUBR1, mitosis runs unsupervised: in reported patients, a quarter
to half of cells carry random chromosome gains and losses. Growth fails, cancer risk rises, and a
progeroid-like decline sets in.

**S3 (0:40–1:05)**
Replacing a missing checkpoint protein looks classically undruggable. But the evidence on this allele
class points elsewhere: kinase-domain missense mutants still function if the protein simply survives.
The lesion is about abundance, not catalysis — and abundance, in principle, can be raised.

**S4 (1:05–1:35)**
So we built the whole path from variant to phenotype as a graded evidence ledger: every claim tagged
fact, inference, or hypothesis; every claim source-cited; conflicts kept visible, never smoothed over.
Then one disciplined question: which already market-approved drugs act on nodes of this chain?
Approved products only, verified at product level — and every candidate carries its own
counter-evidence.

**S5 (1:35–2:10)**
The result: two research hypotheses on independent axes. Everolimus — an mTORC1 inhibitor with long
pediatric experience in tuberous sclerosis — targets the degenerative branch. No MVA data exist; we
say so. Metformin is explicitly exploratory. Chloroquine we deliberately did not nominate: it could
harm the very tissues this disease damages. And the most mechanistically interesting axis — raising
BUBR1 through NAD–SIRT2 — has no approved single-ingredient product. We excluded it. Rules are rules.

**S6 (2:10–2:30)**
These are research hypotheses, not treatment proposals. Each carries a falsifiable prediction,
testable in a low-cost worm model of checkpoint weakening. And experiment zero comes before
everything: measure BUBR1 abundance in the child's own cells. If it's normal, our central premise
fails — and we would report that.

**S7 (2:30–2:50)**
The framework is disease-agnostic: controlled genotype, graded mechanism chain, approved-drug
screen, falsification first. We walked it end-to-end on a second rare disease as a retrospective
control. It runs — and the hard parts stay honestly hard.

**S8 (2:50–3:00)**
One child. Zero trials. Two testable hypotheses, and a public map of what we don't know. To the
child and family who made this possible: thank you. — JiuTian Bio.

## 4. 制作管线（已选：HTML 屏录）

**主路径：`submissions/track2/pitch-deck.html`**（自包含单文件、无外部依赖、离线可用；
1920×1080 固定舞台自适应缩放；12 页 = §3 分镜，结构校验通过）
1. 双击打开 → 按 **F** 全屏。
2. 彩排：按 **C** 呼出控制条（←/→ 翻页；**A** = 按各页目标时长自动轮播，总 179 s）；
   对照 §2 逐字稿走 2–3 遍，目标成片 ≤2:55。
3. 正式录制（一条过）：QuickTime「新建屏幕录制」全屏 + 同步朗读，点击画面右半或 →
   翻页。分轨备选：先录旁白音轨（Voice Memos），再屏录无声版，ffmpeg 对齐合成。
4. `ffprobe` 验时长 ≤3:00（提交前必查）；.srt 按幕时间码（§5 模板）。
5. 上传 YouTube → **Unlisted**；文件名 `JiuTian-Bio_track2_pitch_v1.mp4`；链接入
   `submissions/` 留档 + B2 待插入清单。

**弃用备选（Keynote Record Slideshow）**：HTML 路径不顺利可随时切回——按 §3 建 12 页
一次录完导出 1080p。
