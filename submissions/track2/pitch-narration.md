## 第 1 页 · One child. Zero trials.（~18 s）

Mosaic variegated aneuploidy syndrome is so rare that for the child behind this dataset, there are
zero registered clinical trials. Not few. Zero — re-verified live against the registry the week we
finished this work. This is our three-minute account of what can still be done.

✂ 超时先砍：末句（"This is our three-minute account…"）

## 第 2 页 · BUB1B — the checkpoint gene（~10 s）

In this child, the cause is biallelic loss of BUB1B — the gene encoding BUBR1, a spindle assembly
checkpoint kinase.

（读完停一拍，让链式图停留——下页继续）

## 第 3 页 · What loss looks like（~14 s）

Without enough BUBR1, mitosis runs unsupervised: in reported patients, a quarter to half of cells
carry random chromosome gains and losses. Growth fails, cancer risk rises, and a progeroid-like
decline sets in.

## 第 4 页 · Abundance, not catalysis（~19 s）

Replacing a missing checkpoint protein looks classically undruggable. But the evidence on this
allele class points elsewhere: kinase-domain missense mutants still function if the protein simply
survives. The lesion is about abundance, not catalysis — and abundance, in principle, can be raised.

（"abundance, not catalysis" 处放慢、加重——这是全片核心句）

## 第 5 页 · Every claim graded（~14 s）

So we built the whole path from variant to phenotype as a graded evidence ledger: every claim
tagged fact, inference, or hypothesis; every claim source-cited; conflicts kept visible, never
smoothed over.

## 第 6 页 · Approved products only（~13 s）

Then one disciplined question: which already market-approved drugs act on nodes of this chain?
Approved products only, verified at product level — and every candidate carries its own
counter-evidence.

✂ 可砍："verified at product level"

## 第 7 页 · Two hypotheses — and two honest exclusions（~23 s）

The result: two research hypotheses on independent axes. Everolimus — an mTORC1 inhibitor with
long pediatric experience in tuberous sclerosis — targets the degenerative branch. No MVA data
exist; we say so. Metformin is explicitly exploratory. Chloroquine we deliberately did not
nominate: it could harm the very tissues this disease damages.

（本页信息最密，是全片最容易超时的一页；读表时目光按行走即可，不必逐字指读）

## 第 8 页 · The most interesting axis…（~12 s）

And the most mechanistically interesting axis — raising BUBR1 through NAD–SIRT2 — has no approved
single-ingredient product. We excluded it. Rules are rules.

✂ 超时先砍："Rules are rules."（但它是全片记忆点之一，尽量保留）

## 第 9 页 · Experiment 0（~20 s）

These are research hypotheses, not treatment proposals. Each carries a falsifiable prediction,
testable in a low-cost worm model of checkpoint weakening. And experiment zero comes before
everything: measure BUBR1 abundance in the child's own cells. If it's normal, our central premise
fails — and we would report that.

✂ 可砍："of checkpoint weakening"（压成 "a low-cost worm model"）

## 第 10 页 · Disease-agnostic by design（~17 s）

The framework is disease-agnostic: controlled genotype, graded mechanism chain, approved-drug
screen, falsification first. We walked it end-to-end on a second rare disease as a retrospective
control. It runs — and the hard parts stay honestly hard.

✂ 超时先砍：末分句（"It runs — and the hard parts stay honestly hard."，保留前半到 "control."）
